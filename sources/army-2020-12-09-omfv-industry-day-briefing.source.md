Slide 5 gives the "Updated OMFV Characteristics (CON)" — revised wording that
differs materially from the 25 Feb 2020 release, especially for Survivability
and Manning — and states that the CON is the only requirement document prior to
receipt of industry concepts. Slide 11 lays out the requirements chain. Slide 17
plots seven named analytical efforts (ARIES 1, MBL1, ARIES 2, TRAC AoA, HSI 1,
HSI 2, TRAC OE) on the Phase 2 schedule; slide 18 describes each.
