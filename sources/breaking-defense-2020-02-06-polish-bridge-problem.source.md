The two sentences the argument rests on:

> "And few of them [Eastern European bridges] can handle more than 55 tons."

> "Poland in particular has many rivers and few reinforced bridges."

**Why it matters.** Published nineteen days before the Army released the nine
characteristics, this article lays out a public, articulable operational
rationale for the Poland-bridges assumption that GAO later flagged as
unevidenced. That makes it a *better* worked example, not a worse one: the
assumption is not silly, it is obviously reasonable to anyone who knows the
theatre, and a reviewer would nod past it. Plausibility is not evidence. The
point of the schema is not to catch the absurd claim but to make the reasonable,
load-bearing, unevidenced one impossible to nod past.
