# to identify the capabilities and requirements needed for TWVs in the MDO environment (printed p. 9), to inform the Army's 2022 TWV Strategy (printed pp. 9, 12)

## Status

PLAN_APPROVED

## Context

**In scope:** the TWVs the Army needs for a notional force in a European MDO environment (printed p. 12); about 66 percent of the types of units currently used in the Army (printed p. 12); some surrogate units for those that currently do not exist but may in 2028— such as Strategic Fires Battalions (printed p. 12)
**Out of scope:** —
**Consequences of erroneous output:** The 2022 TWV Strategy sets TWV fleet capabilities and requirements for a force the Army plans to have MDO-capable in a single theater by 2028; a wrong answer buys the wrong number and type of tactical wheeled vehicles for that force. GAO-21-460 does not state the consequences of error; this sentence is a reconstruction from the study's stated purpose (printed pp. 6, 9).

## Decision Drivers

_(primary drivers: Objectives with `priorityRank` <= 3 — a convention, plan 04 ruling R16, not a schema rule)_
- **Mobility. The ability of a unit to move from point A to B using only its own assets, expressed as a percentage.** (obj-mobility, rank None)
- **Launch and Support Platforms. Some TWVs are used as a platform for weapon systems such as the High Mobility Artillery Rocket System or communication systems.** (obj-launch-support, rank None)
- **Distribution and Transportation. The conveyance and delivery of supplies, equipment, and troops; for example, truck companies that deliver water, fuel, ammunition, and food to the battlefield.** (obj-distribution, rank None)

## Considered Options

- **the current TWV fleet** (alt-current-fleet) [candidate]: Army officials told GAO that the initial results of modeling for the Distribution and Transportation line of effort shows the current TWV fleet is sufficient to provide sustainment (printed p. 14).
- **the TWV fleet required for a European MDO environment** (alt-mdo-fleet) [candidate]: The initial results indicated the potential need for an increase in TWVs and associated trailers due to mobility requirements and the need for additional launch and support platforms (printed p. 14). Army officials stated that these results did not reflect the results of modeling and relied, in part, on previously completed analyses.
