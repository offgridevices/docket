# Docket adopts the Bracket mark as its product logo

Date: 2026-09-12. Status: settled.

**Chosen:** the Bracket mark (variant 01 from the icon exploration) as the Docket product
identity, generated from `ui/scripts/gen-docket-logo.mjs` into `ui/src/brand/logo/docket/`.
The app header and browser favicon use Docket; the footer keeps the OffGrid wordmark.

**Over:** continuing to use the OffGrid beacon ring (`mark-ember.svg`) as the Docket
product mark; and hand-maintaining dozens of SVG copies.

**Why.** Docket is a product under OffGrid, not OffGrid itself. The Bracket mark encodes
the product's core UI rule (kernel-computed numerals live inside survey brackets) in a
silhouette that reads at 16 px. A generator script keeps every aspect-ratio variant
aligned with one geometry definition, matching how `gen-types.mjs` prevents schema drift.

**What would reverse it.** A formal brand handoff from OffGrid that specifies a different
Docket mark, or a product rename that makes the bracket metaphor wrong.
